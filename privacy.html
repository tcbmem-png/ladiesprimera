Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2, 2026

Ladies Primera Cleaning LLC ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

  • Contact information: name, email address, phone number

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule and coordinate cleaning services

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services and comply with legal obligations.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing ladiesprimera901@gmail.com.

6. Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page.

8. Contact
